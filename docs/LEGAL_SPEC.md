# LEGAL SPEC

## Required legal/public pages

The website must include clearly accessible links to:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions
- Contact
- Support
- FAQ

### Recommended routes

- `/privacy`
- `/terms`
- `/contact`
- `/support`
- `/faq`

If Terms of Service and Terms & Conditions are legally intended to be the same document, use one canonical `/terms` page and label it clearly as "Terms of Service / Terms & Conditions" rather than maintaining two conflicting copies.

## Legal content requirements

Privacy Policy should address, as applicable:
- information collected
- contact-form data
- analytics
- cookies/local storage
- purposes of processing
- service providers
- retention
- security
- user rights and choices
- children's/privacy considerations where relevant
- changes to the policy
- contact information

Terms should address, as applicable:
- acceptable use
- intellectual property
- website content
- project/service information
- third-party links
- disclaimers
- limitation of liability
- changes
- governing law/jurisdiction where applicable
- contact information

## Important

These are implementation requirements, not legal advice.
Final legal text must be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which Onkai operates and serves users.

Do not invent legal claims, registrations, addresses, corporate entities, or rights.
